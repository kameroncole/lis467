# Massachusetts STE Standards & Youth Engineering Solutions (YES) Alignment

This document provides an overview of the core objectives of the [<u>Massachusetts Science and Technology/Engineering (STE) Curriculum Framework</u>](https://www.doe.mass.edu/stem/ste/standards.html) and maps curriculum offerings from Youth Engineering Solutions (YES) to specific state standards.

## Framework Objectives

- **Emphasizing Conceptual Understanding & Application:** Shifting instruction from passive recall toward active investigation, engineering design prototyping, and real-world problem-solving.

- **Integrating Core Ideas with Practices:** Seamlessly blending core disciplinary knowledge across physical, life, and Earth/space sciences with Science and Engineering Practices (SEPs).

- **Establishing Coherent Learning Progressions:** Developing continuous, age-appropriate STEM progressions from early childhood through middle and high school.

- **Elevating Technological & Engineering Literacy:** Positioning Technology/Engineering as an essential academic discipline to build computational and design thinking.

- **Fostering Career Readiness & Global Civic Engagement:** Equipping students with data literacy, critical analysis skills, and problem-solving habits for post-secondary success.
