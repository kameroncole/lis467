# Museum of Science (Boston) Exhibits: YES Curricula & MA STE Standards Alignment

This document provides a comprehensive mapping of permanent and featured exhibits at the [<u>Museum of Science, Boston</u>](https://www.mos.org/) to learning activities from [<u>Youth Engineering Solutions (YES)</u>](https://yes.mos.org/curricula/) and academic standards from the [<u>Massachusetts Science and Technology/Engineering (STE) Curriculum Framework</u>](https://www.doe.mass.edu/stem/ste/standards.html). Where a specific learning activity or standard does not match an exhibit, it is identified as a **Curriculum Gap**.

## Framework Alignment Summary

- **Engineering & Design Integration:** Exhibits such as the *Engineering Design Workshop* and *Arctic Adventure* map directly to core middle school engineering standards (MA MS-ETS1 series).

- **Computer Science & AI Expansion:** Cutting-edge exhibits like *Exploring AI* and *Gaia Globe* align with YES Computer Science companion modules (e.g., *Photo Classification*, *Heatwave Visualizations*) and MA Digital Literacy & CS standards.

- **Identified Curriculum Gaps:** YES currently emphasizes engineering design and CS integration; museum exhibits focused on paleontology (*Colossal Fossil*, *Dinosaurs*), rotational earth dynamics (*Foucault Pendulum*), and zoological husbandry (*Live Animal Care Center*) represent curriculum gaps within the YES catalog, though they align fully with MA STE content standards.
